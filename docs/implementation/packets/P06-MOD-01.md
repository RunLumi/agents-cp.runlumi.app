## Outcome
Implement pure canonical schedule, occurrence identity, missed/overlap, lease, and off-peak semantics with deterministic tests.

## In scope
- `ScheduleRule` parsing/canonicalization and timezone/DST handling.
- Stable occurrence identity and manual-run identity.
- Overlap, missed-run, reconnect/catch-up, pause/suspend, and lease-expiry decisions.
- Off-peak safety policy composition without flattening ZCode semantics.

## Explicitly out of scope
- D1 repositories, HTTP handlers, queue transport, or LumiAgents execution.
- Billing, webhook delivery, or data-governance jobs.

- events: P06 automation event names.
- policy schema: P06 automation/off-peak sections.

## Implementation notes
Keep schedule calculation independent of Worker time and timezone libraries that do not compile for WASM. Tests must use fixed instants and include DST, long-offline, reconnect, overlap, and device-loss cases.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Same logical schedule instant always produces the same occurrence ID.
- [ ] Redelivery/retry cannot create another logical occurrence.
- [ ] Overlap and missed policies are deterministic and bounded.
- [ ] Lease expiry permits a new attempt without duplicating the occurrence.
- [ ] Off-peak restrictions cannot be broadened by schedule data.
