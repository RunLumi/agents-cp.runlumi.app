## Outcome
Authorize and route one inference request by alias, resolve credentials safely, dispatch with timeout, stream without buffering, reconcile usage/audit, and never fall back after commitment.

## In scope
- Native and OpenAI-compatible endpoints.
- SSE response construction, backpressure, cancellation/timeout handling.
- Usage event and budget hook integration.

## Explicitly out of scope
- Full P05 run/tool execution and P06 billing.
- Provider-specific public response shapes beyond the supported subset.
