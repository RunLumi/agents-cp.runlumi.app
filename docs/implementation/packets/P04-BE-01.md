## Outcome
Expose tenant-scoped catalog and immutable route draft/publish/history/rollback APIs through the central authorization bridge.

## In scope
- Catalog/provider/model/alias reads and admin mutations.
- Route create/publish/history/rollback with idempotency and version checks.
- D1 repositories and audit/outbox statements.

## Explicitly out of scope
- Plaintext credential resolution and upstream inference.
