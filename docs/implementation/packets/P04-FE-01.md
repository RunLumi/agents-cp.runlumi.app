## Outcome
Provide a dense, keyboard-reachable Models & Routing view for catalog lifecycle, capabilities, aliases, and provider health.

## In scope
- Catalog table/filter states and route alias presentation.
- Loading, empty, error, permission-denied, and disabled states.

## Explicitly out of scope
- Full billing dashboards, graph editor, and client secret reveal.
