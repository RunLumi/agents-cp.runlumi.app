## Outcome
Deliver durable notification projections and signed, retryable HTTPS webhook delivery with stable event IDs, bounded backoff, dead-letter, replay, and auto-disable.

## In scope
- Webhook endpoint/secret storage and rotation statements.
- Delivery projection, signature generation, retry/replay endpoints.
- Queue consumer/handler integration and stable failure codes; do not reuse request-bound auth-code email delivery for durable notifications.
- Notification preferences and in-app notification projection.

## Explicitly out of scope
- Automation schedule semantics, billing, or data export/deletion.
- Logging raw payloads or returning webhook secrets after creation.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Business mutation succeeds when a destination is down after durable enqueue.

- [ ] Signature and replay-window verification are deterministic.
- [ ] Dead-letter/replay and optional auto-disable are auditable.
- [ ] Secrets and raw confidential payloads never enter logs or responses.
