## Outcome
Provide a form-based route editor with candidates, weights/order, timeout/retry settings, publish history, rollback, and health indicators.

## In scope
- Route list/detail, draft validation, publish/rollback confirmation.
- Version history and selected-provider health presentation.

## Explicitly out of scope
- Node graph, arbitrary JavaScript rules, and secret editing.
