## Outcome
Allow authorized users to create, inspect masked, rotate, and revoke provider credentials without redisplaying secrets.

## In scope
- Credential metadata list and create/rotate/revoke flows.
- Clear one-time input, validation, busy, success, and error states.

## Explicitly out of scope
- Reveal, export, copy-after-save, or local persistence of plaintext secrets.
